Rex Vane is back in Sunport, and Mara Quinn owns his debt: $50,000. Five jobs. One city. Steal cars, outrun the SPPD, and pay it all off — or go down trying.

WASD / arrows — walk or drive  ·  Shift — run
E — get in / out of a car  ·  Space — handbrake
F — punch  ·  Mouse — aim, click to shoot
Enter — start the next mission  ·  T — take a taxi fare
Esc — pause  ·  M — mute
Stealing cars, hitting pedestrians and shooting all raise your wanted level. Lose the cops, lay low, and the heat fades.
An original game. Sunport, Rex Vane and Mara Quinn are invented for this game — no part of it comes from any commercial game.
